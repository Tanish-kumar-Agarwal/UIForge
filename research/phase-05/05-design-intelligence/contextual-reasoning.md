## 1. The 10-Step Design Reasoning Pipeline

UIForge executes design intelligence through a 10-step pipeline:

1. **Step 1: Product & Persona Understanding:** Extract target domain, primary user persona, task frequency, and device constraints from the design brief.
2. **Step 2: Purpose & Intent Identification:** Determine whether the screen is for consumption (editorial), operational manipulation (SaaS/data grid), or transaction (checkout/payment).
3. **Step 3: Information & Hierarchy Mapping:** Parse content units into primary focal point ($L_1$), secondary groupings ($L_2$), and metadata ($L_3$).
4. **Step 4: Existing Convention Audit (Workflow B):** When modifying an existing project, analyze AST to extract existing typography pairings, token ramps, and layout structures to preserve brand identity.
5. **Step 5: Faceted Knowledge Retrieval:** Query the design knowledge index for normative rules, positive patterns, and applicable anti-patterns.
6. **Step 6: Multi-Alternative Pattern Comparison:** Evaluate competing candidate layouts (e.g. data table vs card deck vs split screen) against domain trade-offs.
7. **Step 7: Proactive Anti-Pattern Screening:** Actively check proposed layouts against the 32+ rules in `anti-pattern-catalogue.json`.
8. **Step 8: Accessibility & Physical Ergonomics Verification:** Validate contrast (4.5:1), touch targets (44px), focus rings, and optical measure (65ch).
9. **Step 9: Conflict Resolution & Rule Precedence:** Resolve trade-offs (e.g. density vs touch target size) using the formal Precedence Hierarchy.
10. **Step 10: Traceable Synthesis & Artifact Generation:** Output compiled DTCG tokens, component AST, and an explainable design decision log citing exact `EVID-*` references.
