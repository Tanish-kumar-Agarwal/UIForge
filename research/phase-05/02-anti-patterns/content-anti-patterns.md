## 1. Catalog of Content Anti-Patterns

### `ANTI-CNT-001`: Generic Placeholder Copy & Unrealistic Marketing Claims
- **Description:** Using Latin "Lorem ipsum" in production, hyperbolic buzzwords ("Quantum AI Synergy"), or absurd fake statistics ("10,000% ROI").
- **Symptom:** Total loss of user credibility; lack of concrete product explanation.
- **Root Defect:** Generative AI hallucinating marketing cliches rather than articulating concrete product workflows (`EVID-SYS-005`).
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Remediation:** Write concise, domain-specific copy that describes the concrete problem solved and the exact workflow supported.
- **Positive Counterpart:** `PATT-MKT-001`.

### `ANTI-CNT-002`: Fake Testimonials & Stock AI Avatars
- **Description:** Generating 3 fictional testimonials with hyper-polished AI generated portrait headshots and vague praise ("This tool changed my life!").
- **Symptom:** Instantly signals inauthenticity; destroys enterprise buyer trust.
- **Root Defect:** Superficial template filler.
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Remediation:** Use real customer case studies with specific metrics, or replace the section with verifiable architecture diagrams and documentation snippets.
- **Positive Counterpart:** `PATT-ENT-001`.

### `ANTI-CNT-003`: Empty Visual Sections (Decorative Shells Without Workflow Purpose)
- **Description:** Rendering feature sections, cards, or hero graphic containers that contain elaborate decorative illustrations but zero actionable information or workflow utility.
- **Symptom:** Wasted viewport real estate; users scan past meaningless decorative fluff trying to find real product capabilities.
- **Root Defect:** Treating interfaces as static graphic canvases rather than functional problem-solving software (`EVID-HCI-001`, `EVID-SYS-005`).
- **Severity:** `MEDIUM`
- **Remediation:** Every visual card or section must contain actionable product data, verified capability descriptions, or interactive previews.
- **Positive Counterpart:** `PATT-MKT-001`, `PATT-ENT-001`.

### `ANTI-CNT-004`: Mismatched Domain Copy & Tone Dissonance
- **Description:** Using playful consumer slang, celebratory confetti copy ("Woohoo!"), or vague marketing hype inside high-acuity financial, clinical, or administrative interfaces.
- **Symptom:** Undermines trust; causes severe cognitive dissonance during high-stress operational or emergency tasks.
- **Root Defect:** Ignoring domain context and user emotional posture in microcopy engineering (`EVID-SYS-004`, `EVID-HCI-002`).
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Remediation:** Align tone strictly with domain: objective, precise, and sober language in enterprise/health/fintech; save playful elements for consumer entertainment contexts.
- **Positive Counterpart:** `PATT-HLT-001`, `PATT-ADM-001`.
