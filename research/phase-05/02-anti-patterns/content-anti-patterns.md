## 1. Catalog of Content Anti-Patterns

### `ANTI-CNT-001`: Generic Placeholder Copy & Unrealistic Marketing Claims
- **Description:** Using Latin "Lorem ipsum" in production, hyperbolic buzzwords ("Quantum AI Synergy"), or absurd fake statistics ("10,000% ROI").
- **Symptom:** Total loss of user credibility; lack of concrete product explanation.
- **Root Defect:** Generative AI hallucinating marketing cliches rather than articulating concrete product workflows (`EVID-SYS-005`).
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Remediation:** Write concise, domain-specific copy that describes the concrete problem solved and the exact workflow supported.
- **Positive Counterpart:** `PATT-MKT-001`.

### `ANTI-CNT-002`: Fake Testimonials & Stock AI Avatars
- **Description:** Generating 3 fictional testimonials with hyper-polished AI generated portrait headshots and vague praise ("This tool changed my life!").
- **Symptom:** Instantly signals inauthenticity; destroys enterprise buyer trust.
- **Root Defect:** Superficial template filler.
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Remediation:** Use real customer case studies with specific metrics, or replace the section with verifiable architecture diagrams and documentation snippets.
- **Positive Counterpart:** `PATT-ENT-001`.
