## 1. Executive Mandate: Eliminating Semantic Ambiguity

A primary source of defects in AI coding and team collaboration is ambiguous terminology (e.g. confusing "contrast ratio" with "color brightness", or "line height" with "vertical rhythm").

This glossary establishes the **immutable definitions** enforced across all UIForge contracts, schemas, AST analyzers, and documentation.

---

## 2. Canonical Terms & Definitions

### A
- **Affordance:** The actionable property between an object and an actor (e.g. a physical button afford pressing). In UI, visual signifiers communicate affordances.
- **Alignment:** The precise spatial positioning of elements along common horizontal, vertical, or baseline axes.
- **Anti-Pattern:** A recognizable implementation pattern or visual trope that reliably produces usability defects, accessibility failures, or maintenance debt.
- **APCA (Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm):** A modern method for calculating text contrast based on human spatial vision and spatial frequency, proposed for WCAG 3.0.

### B
- **Baseline Grid:** A repetitive vertical rhythm grid (typically 4px or 8px) to which typographic baselines and element heights snap.
- **Bento Grid:** A modular layout composed of asymmetric rectangular containers ($1\times1$, $2\times1$, $2\times2$) grouping discrete features.

### C
- **Chroma ($C$):** In OKLCH color space, the colorfulness or purity of a hue relative to neutral grey of identical lightness.
- **Chunking:** The cognitive process of grouping individual pieces of information into meaningful, cohesive units ($4 \pm 1$ items).
- **Common Region:** A Gestalt principle stating that elements bounded within the same visual boundary or background surface are perceived as a group.
- **Container Query:** A CSS standard (`@container`) enabling a component to adapt its layout based on the width of its parent container rather than the global viewport.

### D
- **Design Token:** A machine-readable key-value pair storing design attributes (color, typography, spacing) with a designated type, name, and semantic intent.
- **DTCG:** Design Tokens Community Group under the W3C, author of the authoritative standard design token exchange format.

### E
- **Elevated Surface:** A UI surface that appears physically higher than the canvas, communicated via drop shadow (light mode) or lighter surface tint (dark mode).
- **Expressive Mode:** A design system state characterized by generous whitespace, larger typography, and brand motion, optimized for engagement and narrative.

### F
- **Fitts's Law:** A human-computer interaction model predicting that the time required to rapidly move to a target area is a function of the ratio between distance and target width.
- **Focus Ring:** A high-contrast visual outline displayed around an active interactive element during keyboard navigation.

### G
- **Gestalt Principles:** Psychological laws of perceptual grouping (proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, common fate, common region).

### H
- **Harmonic Type Scale:** A sequence of font sizes generated by multiplying a base size by a constant musical interval ratio (e.g. 1.200 Minor Third, 1.250 Major Third).

### L
- **Leading (Line Height):** The vertical distance between the baselines of successive lines of type.
- **Luminance Contrast:** The relative brightness ratio between two colors calculated using standard photometric equations (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3).

### M
- **Measure:** The horizontal length of a line of text, measured in characters or typographic units (`ch`). Optimal measure is 45-75 characters.
- **Microinteraction:** A subtle, contained product interaction revolving around a single use case (e.g. toggling a switch, pull-to-refresh).

### O
- **OKLCH:** A cylindrical color space modeling Lightness, Chroma, and Hue designed for perceptual uniformity matching human vision.
- **Optimistic UI:** An architectural pattern where client state updates instantaneously before the supporting network request completes, with deterministic rollback on error.

### P
- **Preattentive Processing:** The subconscious, rapid (<200ms) accumulation of visual information across spatial coordinates, colors, and sizes prior to conscious attention.
- **Productive Mode:** A design system state characterized by high data density, compact row heights (32-40px), and minimal motion, optimized for high-frequency operational tasks.
- **Proximity:** The Gestalt principle stating that objects positioned close together are perceived as belonging to the same functional group.

### S
- **Salience:** The distinct optical prominence of an element relative to its surroundings, commanding visual attention.
- **State Layer:** A semi-transparent overlay (8%, 12%, 16%) applied over a component surface to communicate interaction states (hover, focus, pressed) consistently across themes.
- **Subgrid:** A CSS Grid Level 2 feature (`grid-template-rows: subgrid`) allowing nested child grids to participate directly in the parent grid's track sizing.

### T
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** Numeric glyphs where every digit (0-9) occupies identical horizontal width, ensuring vertical decimal alignment in columns.
- **Tracking (Letter Spacing):** The uniform optical spacing applied across an entire string or paragraph of characters.

### V
- **Variable Font:** An OpenType font format storing an entire family of weights, widths, and slants in a single compact file interpolating across continuous axes.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** The optical arrangement of elements in descending order of communicative importance.
