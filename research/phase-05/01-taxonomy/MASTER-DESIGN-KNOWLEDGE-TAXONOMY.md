## 3. Cross-Domain Mapping (Applicability Across 10 Domains)

Every entry in the taxonomy is mapped against UIForge's 10 canonical product domains:
1. **Editorial Websites:** High emphasis on measure (65ch), serifs, vertical cadence, reading flow.
2. **Enterprise SaaS:** High emphasis on 2-tier navigation, app shell, productive density, keyboard shortcuts.
3. **Fintech:** Non-negotiable tabular numerals, right-aligned decimals, strict confirmation dialogs.
4. **E-Commerce:** Resource lists, index tables, actionable empty states, clear checkout funnels.
5. **Developer Tools:** Monospace code optimization, high-contrast dark themes, dense information hierarchy.
6. **Healthcare:** Accessible Atkinson/IBM Plex typography, zero ambiguous dosage figures, error prevention.
7. **Education Platforms:** Progressive disclosure, bite-sized chunking, multi-modal feedback.
8. **Data Dashboards:** Card decks with subgrid alignment, categorical color palettes, KPI hierarchy.
9. **Administrative Systems:** GOV.UK style radical clarity, error summaries, accessible forms.
10. **Consumer Applications:** Touch ergonomics (44px targets), expressive animations, fluid microinteractions.
